About Me

Hi, I’m Daniel Owolabi, a UI/UX designer, web developer, and data analyst enthusiast based in Nigeria. I work across design and technology, combining creative thinking with practical problem-solving to build digital experiences that are clear, useful, and purposeful.

Over the past four years, I’ve worked on different digital projects, from mobile app interfaces and responsive websites to brand designs and web-based tools. My experience spans UI/UX design, frontend development, graphics design, data analysis, and Python programming. I’m also exploring AI automation and browser extension development, where design and functionality come together.

What I bring to a project goes beyond making something look good. I pay attention to how people interact with a product, how a business communicates its value, and how technology can make a process simpler. Whether I’m working in Figma, building with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, or using data to find insights, I approach each project with curiosity, attention to detail, and a focus on what the work needs to achieve.

I’m always learning, experimenting with new ideas, and improving how I work. I enjoy collaborating with people who care about what they are building, and I’m open to projects that allow me to contribute through design, development, problem-solving, or a combination of these skills.
